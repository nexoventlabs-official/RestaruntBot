## Step 1: Create Facebook Personal Account

> **Note:** You need a personal Facebook account to manage business assets. If you already have one, skip to Step 2.

### 1.1 Sign Up for Facebook

1. **Go to Facebook:**
   - Open browser and visit: `https://www.facebook.com`

2. **Click "Create New Account"**
   - Green button on the homepage

3. **Fill Registration Form:**

   | Field | What to Enter | Example |
   |-------|--------------|---------|
   | First Name | Your real first name | `Rahul` |
   | Last Name | Your real last name | `Sharma` |
   | Mobile or Email | **Use your PERSONAL mobile/email** | `rahul.sharma1990@gmail.com` OR `+91 98765 43210` |

   | Date of Birth | Your real DOB | `15 Jan 1990` |
   | Gender | Select your gender | `Male` |

   > ⚠️ **Use your PERSONAL email/mobile here, NOT business email**

4. **Click "Sign Up"**

5. **Verify Your Account:**
   - Check your PERSONAL email/SMS for verification code
   - You'll receive a 6-digit code like: `482951`
   - Enter the code and click "Continue"

### 1.2 Complete Profile Setup

1. **Add Profile Picture:**
   - Click on profile icon (top right)
   - Click "Add Profile Picture"
   - Upload YOUR real photo (not business logo)
   - Click "Save"

2. **Add Basic Information:**
   - Go to "About" section
   - Add: Work, Education, Location
   - Make profile look legitimate (Meta checks this)

3. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (IMPORTANT):**
   - Click profile icon → Settings & Privacy → Settings
   - Click "Security and Login"
   - Scroll to "Two-Factor Authentication"
   - Click "Use two-factor authentication"
   - Choose "Text Message (SMS)"
   - Enter your **PERSONAL mobile**: `+91 98765 43210`
   - Receive OTP and verify
   - **Save backup codes** in safe place

### 1.3 Strengthen Your Account (Important for Business Manager)

1. **Add Friends:**
   - Add at least 10-20 friends (real people)
   - This makes your account look legitimate

2. **Be Active for 2-3 Days:**
   - Like some posts
   - Share content
   - Comment on posts
   - Meta prefers established accounts for business

3. **Verify Email and Phone:**
   - Settings → General → Contact
   - Add and verify both:
     - Email: `rahul.sharma1990@gmail.com` ✓
     - Phone: `+91 98765 43210` ✓

   - `https://business.facebook.com/settings`

2. **Click "Security Center":**
   - In left sidebar

3. **Click "Start Verification":**
   - Under "Business Verification" section
   - OR it may appear as a banner at top

4. **Choose Verification Method:**
   - Select: **"Verify with documents"** (recommended)

### 6.2 Prepare Your Documents

**Before uploading, ensure:**

✅ **MSME/Udyam Certificate:**
| Requirement | Check |
|------------|-------|
| Clear, readable scan or photo | ✓ |
| All corners visible | ✓ |
| No glare or shadows | ✓ |
| File format | PDF, JPG, or PNG |
| Max size | 8MB |
| Shows business name | `Tasty Bites Food Services` |
| Shows address | `Shop No. 5, Sunshine Complex...` |
| Shows registration number | `UDYAM-MH-12-0012345` |

✅ **Utility Bill:**
| Requirement | Check |
|------------|-------|
| Recent (within last 3 months) | ✓ |
| Business name clearly visible | ✓ |
| Business address matches MSME | ✓ |
| Clear scan/photo | ✓ |
| Accepted types | Electricity, Water, Gas, Internet, Phone |

1. **Document Upload Screen:**

2. **Primary Document - MSME Certificate:**

   | Field | What to Select/Enter |
   |-------|---------------------|

   | Field | What to Select/Enter |
   |-------|---------------------|
   | Document Type | `Utility Bill` OR `Business Address Proof` |

4. **Additional Document (if requested):**
   - Upload bank statement or rent agreement

   | Field | What to Enter | Example |

   | Business Email | Business email | `tastybites.orders@gmail.com` |
   | Business Website | If available | `https://tastybites.com` |

   > ⚠️ **Phone number here is for OTP verification - use your PERSONAL mobile that you have access to!**

```

Day 2-5: Under Review (Meta reviews documents)
   ↓
```

## Troubleshooting & Common Issues

### Issue 1: Business Verification Rejected

**Reasons:**
- Documents not clear/readable
- Business name mismatch between documents
- Address mismatch between documents
- Documents expired or too old
- Suspicious activity on account

**Solutions:**
1. Review rejection email carefully
2. Fix mentioned issues
3. Ensure ALL documents have EXACT same:
   - Business name: `Tasty Bites Food Services` (not "Tasty Bites" or "TastyBites")
   - Address: `Shop No. 5, Sunshine Complex...` (not "Shop 5" or abbreviated)
4. Use high-quality scans (300 DPI)
5. Wait 30 days before reapplying
6. Try different document combinations

### Issue 2: Phone Number Already Registered

**Error:** "This phone number is already registered on WhatsApp"

**This happens when:** You tried to use your personal number or a number that has WhatsApp

**Solutions:**

1. **If using personal number - STOP!** 
   - Get a NEW SIM card
   - Use that number instead

2. **If the NEW number somehow has WhatsApp:**
   - Open WhatsApp on that phone
   - Go to: Settings → Account → Delete Account
   - Enter phone number: `+91 87654 32109`
   - Click Delete
   - Wait 24 hours
   - Try registering again on Business API

### Issue 3: Display Name Rejected

**Reasons:**
- Name too generic (e.g., "Restaurant" instead of "Tasty Bites Restaurant")
- Name contains special characters
- Name misleading or doesn't match documents
- Name violates policies

**Solutions:**
1. Choose more specific name: `Tasty Bites Restaurant` not `Restaurant`
2. Remove special characters: `Tasty Bites` not `Tasty-Bites™`
3. Match business documents
4. Provide clear explanation
5. Submit additional proof (signage photos, business cards)

### Issue 4: Can't Access Business Manager

**Solutions:**
1. Clear browser cache (Ctrl + Shift + Delete)
2. Try different browser (Chrome recommended)
3. Disable ad blockers
4. Check if Facebook account restricted
5. Verify email and phone on Facebook
6. Enable 2FA on Facebook account

### Issue 5: Verification Taking Too Long

**If more than 7 days:**
1. Check Business Manager for updates
2. Look for "More Info Needed" status
3. Check spam folder for Meta emails
4. Contact Meta Support:
   - Go to: `https://www.facebook.com/business/help/support`
   - Create support ticket
   - Provide: Business ID, submission date, screenshots

### Issue 6: Documents Not Accepted

**Solutions:**
1. Ensure documents show:
   - Business name clearly
   - Business address
   - Issue date (within 3 months for utility bills)
   - Official stamps/signatures

2. Try different document combinations:
   
   | Combination | Primary | Supporting |
   |------------|---------|------------|
   | Option 1 | MSME Certificate | Electricity Bill |
   | Option 2 | MSME Certificate | Bank Statement |
   | Option 3 | MSME + GST | Rent Agreement |

3. Get documents notarized if repeatedly rejected

### Issue 7: WhatsApp API Not Sending Messages

**Checklist:**

| Check | Status |
|-------|--------|
| Display name verified? | ☐ |
| Phone number verified? | ☐ |
| Access token valid (not expired)? | ☐ |
| Message template approved? | ☐ |
| Within message limits (250/day initially)? | ☐ |
| Webhook configured correctly? | ☐ |

**Solutions:**
1. Verify all credentials in `.env` are correct
2. Check API logs for error messages
3. Test with Meta's API testing tool in WhatsApp Manager
4. Ensure message templates are approved before using
5. Check you haven't exceeded message tier limits
6. Verify webhook URL is accessible (not blocked by firewall)

### Issue 8: OTP Not Received

**For Facebook/Business Verification OTP:**
- Check spam/other folders
- Wait 2-3 minutes
- Request "Resend OTP"
- Try voice call option
- Ensure phone has network signal

**For WhatsApp API Number Verification:**
- Ensure NEW number has SIM inserted
- Phone should be ON and receiving SMS
- Try voice call option
- Check with carrier if SMS blocked

## Important Tips & Best Practices

### ✅ DO's

1. **Use Real Business Information:**
   - Never use fake documents
   - Ensure all info matches across ALL platforms
   - Example: If MSME says "Tasty Bites Food Services", use EXACTLY that everywhere

2. **Keep Documents Ready:**
   - Have multiple document options ready
   - Keep digital copies in a dedicated folder
   - Recommended folder structure:
     ```
     Business_Documents/
     ├── MSME_Certificate.pdf
     ├── GST_Certificate.pdf
     ├── Electricity_Bill_Dec2025.pdf
     ├── Bank_Statement_Dec2025.pdf
     └── Rent_Agreement.pdf
     ```

3. **Maintain Active Presence:**
   - Post on Facebook Page 2-3 times per week
   - Respond to messages within 24 hours
   - Keep business info updated

4. **Enable Security Features:**
   - Always use 2FA on Facebook account
   - Use strong, unique passwords
   - Add backup admin to Business Manager
   - Save backup codes securely

5. **Be Patient:**
   - Verification takes 3-7 business days
   - Don't submit multiple times
   - Wait for Meta's response

6. **Document Everything:**
   - Save all confirmation emails
   - Screenshot important steps
   - Note submission dates:
     ```
     Business Verification submitted: 15 Jan 2026
     Display Name submitted: 20 Jan 2026
     ```

### ❌ DON'Ts

1. **Don't Rush:**
   - Take time to fill forms correctly
   - Double-check before submitting
   - Rushing leads to rejections

2. **Don't Mix Personal and Business:**
   
   | Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
   |---------|---------|
   | Using personal email for Business Manager | Use `business@gmail.com` |
   | Using personal phone for WhatsApp API | Use new dedicated number |

3. **Don't Change Info During Verification:**
   - Keep business details consistent
   - Don't edit Facebook page during review
   - Don't change address or name mid-verification

4. **Don't Violate Policies:**
   - Read Meta's commerce policies
   - Follow WhatsApp business policies
   - No spam, prohibited content, or fake info

5. **Don't Share Access Tokens:**
   - Keep credentials secure
   - Never commit `.env` to GitHub
   - Use environment variables only
   - Never share tokens on WhatsApp/chat
